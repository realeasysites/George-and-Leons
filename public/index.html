What's a proper North Shore roast beef?

We'll tell you.

On the North Shore of Massachusetts, the roast beef sandwich isn't a menu item — it's a lifestyle. Our classic, formally known as a Three Way, is stacked like this:

  1. 1
    Wicked rare beef roasted in-house and shaved super thin.
  2. 2
    White American melting into the heat.
  3. 3
    James River BBQ — the North Shore sauce. Murder it with extra and let it roll down your arms.
  4. 4
    Duke's mayo on a griddled onion roll. That's the whole deal.

Want lettuce? We don't really do that. No need to mess with perfection. Pickles? Fine. We can talk about it.

From the North Shore to Westbrook

A food truck that got too big for its wheels.

Founder Logan Abbey grew up in Newburyport, Massachusetts, where a roast beef sandwich is part of everyday life. After moving to Portland, he noticed the city's food scene was missing the beefy magic he grew up on — so he set out to fix it.

In 2020 he started with pop-ups at local restaurants and breweries, perfected the sandwich, and rolled out the George's North Shore food truck that August. The Three Way became a cult favorite, and in 2023 the beef got a permanent home at 9 Cumberland Street in Westbrook.

The name? George is Logan's dog — a Chihuahua-pug mix named after the iconic, classically handsome George Costanza. Leon is Logan's son. Put them together and you've got the family business.
